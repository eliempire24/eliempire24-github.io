02 Coverage & Criticism Austin Film Festival

Reading the Story Beneath the Story

For over a year I have read and evaluated competition screenplays for the Austin Film Festival, writing coverage that identifies what works, what stalls, and whether a script should advance.

My notes are known for being thorough, structural, and constructive. I name the problem and offer the fix. The same eye drives my film and television criticism.
